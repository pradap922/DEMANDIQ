💡 About the Project

Traditional inventory planning often depends on manual estimation and past experience, which can lead to stock shortages or overstocking. DemandIQ solves this problem by applying intelligent forecasting models that learn patterns from historical data.

The platform integrates Prophet for time-series forecasting, XGBoost for advanced ML-based demand analysis, interactive dashboards for visual insights, and an AI Chat Assistant for real-time explanations. This creates a smart environment where users not only see predictions but also understand them.
